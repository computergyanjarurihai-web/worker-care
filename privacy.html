Privacy Policy

Aapki jaankari surakshit rakhna hamari zimmedari hai

Last updated: August 2026

1. Hum Kya Jaankari Ekatrit Karte Hain

Jab aap Worker Care par sign up karte hain ya humari services ka upyog karte hain, hum kuch personal jaankari le sakte hain, jaise:

  • Aapka naam, phone number, aur email address
  • Aapka passport/Emirates ID details (jab documents seva ke liye zaroori ho)
  • Aapki location aur employment details
  • Payment aur subscription se judi jaankari

2. Jaankari Ka Upyog

Hum aapki jaankari sirf in kaamon ke liye upyog karte hain:

  • Aapko services provide karne ke liye (helpline, legal help, documents seva, etc.)
  • Aapke account aur subscription ko manage karne ke liye
  • Emergency situations mein turant sahayata dene ke liye
  • Service improvement aur customer support ke liye

3. Jaankari Ki Suraksha

Hum industry-standard security measures ka upyog karte hain taaki aapki jaankari unauthorized access, loss, ya misuse se surakshit rahe. Aapka data encrypted servers par store kiya jaata hai.

4. Jaankari Share Karna

Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi third party ko nahi bechte. Hum aapki jaankari sirf tab share karte hain jab:

  • Aapki sahmati (consent) ho
  • Kanooni zaroorat ho (jaise UAE authorities ki maang par)
  • Aapko service dene ke liye zaroori ho (jaise partner legal advisors ke saath)

5. Aapke Adhikar

Aap kabhi bhi apni jaankari dekhne, update karne, ya delete karwane ki maang kar sakte hain. Iske liye humein support@workercare.ae par sampark karein.

6. Cookies

Hamari website behtar banane ke liye cookies ka upyog kar sakti hai. Aap apne browser settings se cookies ko manage ya band kar sakte hain.

7. Neeti Mein Badlaav

Yeh privacy policy samay-samay par update ho sakti hai. Koi bhi mahatvapurn badlaav is page par post kiya jaayega.

8. Sampark Karein

Privacy se judi kisi bhi jaankari ke liye, humein support@workercare.ae ya +971 62 123 4567 par sampark karein.
