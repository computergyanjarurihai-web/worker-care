Frequently Asked Questions

Aapke sawalon ke jawab yahan milenge

Worker Care kya hai? +
Worker Care UAE mein rehne wale workers aur unke parivar ke liye ek sahayata platform hai. Hum helpline, kanooni salah, dastavez seva, aur emergency madad jaisi services pradaan karte hain.
Main kaise sign up kar sakta hoon? +
Aap website par "Join Now" button par click karke apne liye sahi plan chunkar sign up kar sakte hain. Poora process kuch minute mein poora ho jaata hai.
Helpline kab uplabdh hai? +
Hamari helpline 24 ghante, 7 din uplabdh hai. Aap kabhi bhi +971 62 123 4567 par call kar sakte hain.
Plans ka payment kaise hota hai? +
Payment monthly subscription ke roop mein liya jaata hai. Aap card ya UAE ke local payment methods se pay kar sakte hain.
Kya main apna plan cancel kar sakta hoon? +
Haan, aap kabhi bhi apna subscription cancel kar sakte hain. Cancel karne ke baad, aapka plan current billing cycle ke ant tak active rahega.
Kanooni salah kaise milegi? +
Yogya ya usse upar ke plans mein, aap hamare partner legal advisors se consultation le sakte hain. Helpline par call karke appointment book karein.
Kya family members ko bhi seva mil sakti hai? +
Haan, hamara Family plan poore parivar ke liye welfare support aur assistance shamil karta hai.
Agar mujhe emergency mein madad chahiye toh kya karoon? +
Turant hamari 24x7 helpline par call karein: +971 62 123 4567. Hamari team turant aapki sahayata karegi.

Aur koi sawal hai? support@workercare.ae par likhein ya humein call karein.
